# Editorial Policy

## Ranking neutrality
Listings are not ranked by commercial relationship with Stratum. Search relevance, authority and usability govern discovery.

## Authority
Prefer first-party publisher endpoints. Secondary mirrors may be listed as separate endpoints only when materially useful and clearly identified.

## Public is not open
A publicly searchable registry may still prohibit bulk reuse. `access.type` and `license` are independent.

## Private and paid data
Commercial and controlled sources are welcome when the listing helps a user understand what exists, who controls it, and how access works. The directory does not bypass paywalls, authentication or eligibility controls.

## Descriptions
Write factual, concise descriptions of the data available. Avoid publisher marketing copy.

## Verification
`last_verified` means a human or approved validation workflow confirmed that the resource and described access path still exist. It does not certify data quality or legal fitness for a user's purpose.
