# Code of conduct

Keep discussion about the data, not about the person who raised it.

- Critique an entry, a rule or a decision, and say what would change your mind. "This is wrong, and here is the publisher's own page that says so" is the standard we want. Contempt aimed at whoever wrote the entry is not.
- Assume a contributor knows their own country, language or field better than you do. A `poskod` is not a typo, and a Malay term you have not seen before is not an error.
- Disclose a commercial interest when you review or argue for a listing connected to it. GOVERNANCE.md already requires this of maintainers, and it matters most when the listing is your own.
- No harassment, no discrimination, no sustained disruption, on any ground.

Maintainers enforce this. They can edit, close or revert a contribution and block an account, and will not wait for a warning where the behaviour warrants it.

Report a problem privately rather than in the thread: use the repository's Security tab to open a private report, or message a maintainer on GitHub directly.
