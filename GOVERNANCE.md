# Governance

The directory is initiated and maintained by Stratum Technologies as an open community resource.

Maintainers merge canonical catalogue changes, manage taxonomies and schemas, and enforce editorial policy. Schema/taxonomy changes require maintainer review because they affect every consumer of the metadata.

Material breaking schema changes use a documented migration and schema-version increment. Stable IDs and public URLs should be preserved wherever possible.

Conflicts of interest should be disclosed when a maintainer reviews a listing involving their organization, client or commercial partner. Such relationships must not receive preferential search treatment.
